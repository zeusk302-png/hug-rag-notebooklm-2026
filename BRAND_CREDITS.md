# HUG brand assets and visual references

Reference date: 2026-10-14. This is an educational course companion, not the HUG official website.

- Website: https://www.khug.or.kr/index_hug_in.jsp
- CI and color system: https://www.khug.or.kr/hug/web/ci/kh/cikh000004.jsp

Assets remain attributable to Korea Housing & Urban Guarantee Corporation (HUG), subject to its rights and CI usage rules. They are not Apache-licensed by this project. HUG policy allows free use for materials whose full economic copyright it owns, while excluding third-party protected rights. No broader ownership or permission is inferred for unrelated site photographs or advertising.

The logo and character were not redrawn, recolored or cropped; aspect ratios and original raster bytes are preserved. Character PNG was extracted losslessly from the official introduction HWP's BinData/BIN0002.png stream. Character naming is not asserted. The official color-system and symbol-guide PNGs are retained as reference assets.

Screen color samples from the official color-system image: HUG blue #1C75BC, sky #009EDB, green #80C342, dark blue 1 #004B8F, dark blue 2 #012353. These are sampled screen colors, not a new print-color specification. Darker colors are used for small text to keep contrast readable.

Adaptations to the course: white header, blue chapter selection and buttons, sky informational panels, green decorative accents, generous Korean sans typography, grouped instructional navigation, original character on the home page. Educational labels and scope statements remain visible.
